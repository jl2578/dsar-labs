# M8–M9 synthetic teaching data — v1.0.1

14 November 2026. 5,000 synthetic participants aged 17–18, one synthetic wave. Use `M8_M9_STUDENT.csv` with the dictionary and analysis guide. No real participant-level data are included. This documentation-only update preserves all CSV bytes, names, values and eligibility rules from v1.0.

Blank cells are missing/unusable, not zero. Eligibility differs by task and chosen substance. Group categories are descriptive, not diagnoses. Equal-weight pooled MID measures are instructional derivatives. These observation-scale, single-wave records do not support reliability estimation or temporal/causal claims.

Choose cannabis or alcohol before examining associations. L8 uses Welch for its independent comparison; L9 uses one-way ANOVA and three planned pooled-MSE pairs with Bonferroni adjustment, followed by the fixed stricter-sample sensitivity comparison. See the analysis guide for exact filters and formulas.

The pooled calculations have independent numerical checks and conditional simulation checks for the supplied roster, group sizes and quality structure. These checks do not establish validity for arbitrary real imaging data or certify notebooks and Canvas assessments that are still being built. This is not an exact ABCD release reconstruction.

15 November 2026 wording update: the analysis guide now states the L9 pair directions as lower − zero, higher − zero and higher − lower, matching the Lab 9 notebook. The data, rules and methods are unchanged.

Load identifiers as text and blank cells as missing. Readings and lab materials must use the matching measurement definitions. This local package does not change a Pressbooks chapter or deploy a course.
